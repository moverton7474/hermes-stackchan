# Demo Video Script — M5Stack Partnership

> **Duration:** 60 seconds  
> **Purpose:** Demonstrate the Hermes + AMIE dual-robot setup for M5Stack sales team  
> **Robots:** StackChan #1 (GVC Hermes) + StackChan #2 (AMIE Coach)  
> **Setting:** Milton Overton's executive desk, Visionary AI office  

---

## Pre-Roll Setup

- Both StackChan robots on Milton's desk, angled toward camera
- Robot #1 (Hermes) on left, Robot #2 (AMIE) on right
- Clean desk background showing professional office environment
- Soft overhead lighting; robots' LCD faces clearly visible
- Camera: tripod, eye-level with robots, slightly above desk

---

## Beat 1 — Wake & Greet (0:00–0:10)

**Camera:** Medium shot — Milton at desk with both robots visible. Hermes face shows `idle` (calm eyes, slight gold smile, blinking).

**Milton says:**  
> "Hey Hermes, good morning."

**Hermes reacts:**  
- Face transitions: `idle` → `listening` (eyes widen, gold glow) → `celebrating` (happy eyes, sparkle)
- Head tilts slightly toward Milton

**Hermes responds:**  
> "Good morning, Milton. Ready to have a great day."

**Camera note:** Cut to close-up of Hermes face during the transition. Show the LCD clearly.

---

## Beat 2 — Morning Briefing (0:10–0:20)

**Camera:** Over-the-shoulder shot — Milton looking at Hermes, face clearly visible to camera.

**Hermes face:** `speaking` (animated mouth, confident eyes, gold accents)

**Hermes responds:**  
> "You have the athletics directors meeting at ten, a recruiting call at two, and the Visionary AI board review at four. Top priority: finalize the C-Suite Support chapter edits before noon."

**Milton nods.**

**Camera note:** Brief B-roll insert of the face animation during speech. Keep it tight — the audience should see the mouth moving and expression engaged.

---

## Beat 3 — Team Status (0:20–0:30)

**Milton says:**  
> "Check on the team."

**Hermes face:** `listening` → `thinking` (eyes drift up-right, processing dots animate) → `speaking`

**Hermes responds:**  
> "Claudia just finished the partnership deck. Rex is drafting the investor update. Gemini is analyzing last quarter's coaching metrics. All agents on track."

**Camera:** Tight on Hermes face during the thinking-to-speaking transition. Show the processing dots and gear animation.

---

## Beat 4 — AMIE Coaching (0:30–0:40)

**Camera:** Pan right to Robot #2. AMIE face shows `idle` (warm smile, soft eyes, welcoming).

**Milton says:**  
> "AMIE, coach me on my goals."

**AMIE reacts:**  
- Face transitions: `idle` → `listening` (empathetic, slight tilt) → `coaching` (encouraging eyes, raised brows, motivating smile)

**AMIE responds (coral voice — warm, distinct from Hermes):**  
> "Milton, you're three for four on your weekly habits. You crushed the morning workout and the reading goal. Let's lock in that last one — the gratitude journal — before tonight."

**Camera note:** Show the face difference between Hermes and AMIE. AMIE's expressions should feel warmer, more personal. The voice difference (coral vs marin) should be obvious.

---

## Beat 5 — Goal Celebration (0:40–0:50)

**Milton says:**  
> "Done. Just finished the journal entry."

**AMIE face:** `coaching` → `celebrating` (huge smile, squinted happy eyes, confetti pieces falling, stars sparkling)

**AMIE responds:**  
> "That's what I'm talking about! Four for four this week, Milton. You're building the habits of a champion. Keep this energy!"

**Robot movement:** AMIE does a celebration wiggle (servo pan left-right quickly).

**Camera:** Close-up on AMIE's celebration face with confetti animation. Hold for a beat — this is the money shot.

---

## Beat 6 — Closing (0:50–1:00)

**Camera:** Pull back to wide shot. Both robots side by side on desk, faces visible.

- Hermes shows `idle` (confident, ready)
- AMIE shows `idle` (warm, welcoming)

**Text overlay** (fade in over shot):

```
AMIE Robot Coach
by Visionary AI

Coming 2027
```

**Secondary text** (smaller, below):

```
Powered by StackChan × OpenAI Realtime
visionaryaicoach.com
```

**Milton (voiceover):**  
> "Two robots. One for running the business. One for running your life. That's the vision."

**Hold for 3 seconds. Fade to black.**

---

## Production Notes

### Equipment Needed
- Camera with macro capability (for face close-ups)
- Lapel mic for Milton (clean voice capture)
- The robots' speaker audio should be captured separately (line out or room mic)
- Ring light or soft key light to avoid LCD glare

### Face States Used

| Timestamp | Robot | Face State |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| 0:00 | Hermes | idle |
| 0:03 | Hermes | listening |
| 0:05 | Hermes | celebrating |
| 0:10 | Hermes | speaking |
| 0:20 | Hermes | listening → thinking → speaking |
| 0:30 | AMIE | idle |
| 0:33 | AMIE | listening |
| 0:35 | AMIE | coaching |
| 0:40 | AMIE | celebrating |
| 0:50 | Both | idle |

### Tips
- Run through twice before recording — the Realtime API responses will vary
- Have backup scripted responses ready in case the AI goes off-script
- The face transitions happen automatically based on server emotion tags
- Film the close-ups separately if needed (they can be edited in)
- Total runtime target: 55–65 seconds (tight edit)
